Our mission

Rooted in community.

MercyMed grew from medical outreach in communities across Kyrgyzstan. A permanent clinic became a base for follow-up care, a regular team and continuing medical training.

To improve our patients’ quality of life through competent, compassionate and comprehensive medical care.
