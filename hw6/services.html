Medical services for children and adults. Start with a doctor’s consultation so the next steps fit your needs.

Family medicine

General consultations for adults and families, with local and international doctors.

Family medicine and general care are central to the clinic’s work. The receptionist confirms the appropriate clinician and availability.

02

Pediatrics

Consultations for babies, children and teenagers, including growth and development checkups.

About this service

The clinic has a strong pediatric focus. A consultation comes first; there is no live appointment calendar in this prototype.

03

About this service

Services and clinician availability must be confirmed with the clinic before a visit.

04

Additional examinations

Ultrasound, ECG, vision testing, hearing testing and access to partner laboratory testing.

About this service

These examinations are not booked directly here. Laboratory results are handled by the laboratory; there is no results portal on this website.

05

Preventive care

About this service

The clinic also provides some medical documents for travel and insurance. Ask the clinic to confirm which documents are currently available.

A few helpful answers.

Can I book a test or examination directly?

Please start with a doctor’s consultation. The clinic arranges additional examinations as appropriate.

Is this a live booking website?

No. This is an AUCA Assignment 6 prototype. It does not store patient data, reserve time slots or send requests to the clinic.

Which languages does the website offer?

English, Kyrgyz and Russian. All website copy remains subject to clinic review; Kyrgyz and Russian translations have not yet been checked.
