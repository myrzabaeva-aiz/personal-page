ABOUT

Hi! My name is Aizirek Myrzabaeva. I’m 20 years old, and I’m from Kyrgyzstan. I’m currently a third-year Software Engineering student at the American University of Central Asia. I’m really interested in technology, especially artificial intelligence, data science, and software engineering. I enjoy learning new things, working on different projects, and looking for opportunities that can help me grow both professionally and personally. Besides university, I’m the founder and president of Kyrgyzstan Girls Movement, an organization that works with girls across Kyrgyzstan. Through KGM, I’ve had the opportunity to organize educational programs, campaigns, trainings, and projects focused on girls’ leadership, digital skills, safety, and empowerment. I’m also very interested in international opportunities and meeting people from different countries and backgrounds. I’ve participated in and applied to different international youth programs, conferences, and initiatives. These experiences have made me even more interested in creating projects that can have a real social impact. At the same time, I’m working on developing myself in different areas. I speak Kyrgyz, Russian, and English, and I’m currently learning Korean. I also want to continue developing my skills in AI and technology and gain more international experience. In the future, I want to build a career in technology while continuing to create opportunities for girls and young people in Kyrgyzstan. I have big goals, but for me, the most important thing is to keep learning, trying new things, and turning my ideas into real projects.
